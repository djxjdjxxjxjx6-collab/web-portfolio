Price

All plans include every lesson and the community chat. If the course isn't for you, ask for a full refund in the first 14 days.

Questions

Do I need any experience?

No. Week 1 starts with installing a code editor. If you can use a browser and type, you can start.

What if I fall behind?

Lessons stay open after each week, and you can move to the next cohort once for free.

Which computer do I need?

Any laptop from the last eight years, Windows, macOS or Linux. A tablet won't be enough for the projects.

Will I get a job after this?

Ten weeks makes you a confident beginner, not a senior developer. You'll have five projects to show and a clear idea of what to learn next.
